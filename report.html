Where I’m losing points — focus here

  1. GROUP BY vs ORDER BY. Thought grouping sorts the rows; it actually collapses them into one row per group. 1× — first exposure
    Picture it as: 16 rows in → GROUP BY dept_id → 5 rows out, one per department.
  2. WHERE vs HAVING. Tried filtering an aggregate with WHERE / inside the SELECT list. An aggregate condition can only go in HAVING, which runs after grouping. 1× — self-corrected
  3. Not a concern: both misses are the two most common first-time GROUP BY confusions — not a sign of trouble. Every other watch-item (table-name s, incomplete queries, NULL logic) stayed resolved.expected
